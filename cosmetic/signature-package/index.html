How do I place an order?

Choose a product, select your preferred shade or option, add it to your bag and review your order before checkout.

Can I order more than one product?

Yes. You can add multiple products to your bag and adjust the quantities before checkout.

Can I change my shade or option?

Yes. Each product page clearly shows the available shade or option choices before you add the item to your bag.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on the final shipping method and destination. Confirmed delivery estimates can be displayed here.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange eligibility will follow ZENIOR's store policy. For hygiene-sensitive beauty items, certain conditions may apply.
